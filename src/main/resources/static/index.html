Good to know

Where do you deliver?

Within 8 km of the bakery, usually in 30–45 minutes. Delivery is free over $25.

Do your rolls contain allergens?

They contain gluten, dairy and eggs, and nuts are handled in our kitchen. Each item lists its allergens.

Can I order for an event?

Yes. Boxes of 12 or more need 24 hours' notice. Send us a message on WhatsApp.

How can I pay?

Cash or card at the door or at the counter.

Do you have milk alternatives?

Oat and almond milk for +$0.60 on any coffee.
